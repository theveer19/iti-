WELCOME TO ITI AFFILIATION CONSULTANCY

ITI AFFILIATION CONSULTANCY

ITI Affiliation Consultancy is your single, trusted partner for everything involved in starting and running an Industrial Training Institute. From opening a new ITI and ITI re-affiliation to machine & tools procurement, lab setup, and complete documentation, we manage the entire journey end to end. Our work is carried out under the direct guidance of experienced government and industry professionals, so every file is prepared exactly to the latest QCI, NCVT and DGT norms. You focus on your vision — we handle the process, paperwork and approvals that turn it into a fully affiliated ITI college.

Why choose us: With years of hands-on experience in opening and re-affiliating ITI colleges across Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh and beyond, we deliver complete, norm-compliant files at transparent and affordable processing fees. Our team guides you at every step — saving you time, avoiding costly mistakes, and giving your application the strongest possible chance of approval. With dedicated 24×7 support, expert advice is always just one call away.
